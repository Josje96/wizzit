# Additional Resources

Sources for short daily questions in Wizzit:

- **OpenStax, [Prealgebra 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/prealgebra-2e)** — arithmetic, fractions, ratios, percentages, and practice problems. The content is **CC BY 4.0**, so it can be adapted and reused with attribution.
- **OpenStax, [Contemporary Mathematics](https://openstax.org/details/books/contemporary-mathematics)** — practical math, geometry, finance, and quantitative reasoning. Also **CC BY 4.0**.
- **Illustrative Mathematics, [Tasks](https://tasks.illustrativemathematics.org/)** — classroom problems that can inspire arithmetic, geometry, and reasoning questions. Its curriculum materials are generally **CC BY 4.0**; check the license on individual materials before reusing.
- **Henry Ernest Dudeney, [Amusements in Mathematics](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16713)** — classic logic, number, and spatial puzzles. The 1917 book is public domain in the U.S.; useful for adapting puzzle ideas into short questions.
- **NRICH, University of Cambridge — [resources](https://nrich.maths.org/)** — strong spatial-reasoning and math-puzzle activities. Treat these as **inspiration rather than copyable content** unless a specific item’s terms permit reuse.
- **Khan Academy, [Arithmetic](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/arithmetic)** — clear practice and explanations for basic arithmetic. Good for checking concepts and difficulty; don’t copy its questions or explanations without permission.

For content you plan to publish in the app, OpenStax and public-domain Dudeney puzzles are the clearest starting points. Keep attribution and license records for anything you adapt.
